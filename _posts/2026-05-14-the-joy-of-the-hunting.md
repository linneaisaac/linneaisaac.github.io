I hunt because they hunted for a hundred thousand years. I pass the test, I will diminish. Hear my cry, oh ancestors:


<!--more-->

oh young shaman \
don't go \
for he defeated the spirits \
forever \
that we would never have to be \
yoked to them again

so dance and \
lay down your load \
in the fields, in the forests \
and summon to yourself courage \
and nothing else

for the great dance is a generous way \
and the great dancer \
always watching

Dedicated to a thousand thousand shamans
