Portfolio

A selection of projects I've shipped, solo and in collaboration.

Solo & Claude-Code Projects

On these I own the whole loop — scoping, driving Claude Code to build, working with users, and shipping. High agency and adaptability.

Bentoboard

A values-driven todo board — pick a value (Refresh, Connect, Create…), write a goal, drop it into a bento-shaped slot. Built with my mom as the first user, iterating on the design in real time during a single working session.

The Oracles of Astrampsychus

An interactive version of an ancient Greek oracle book attributed to an Egyptian magus. Pick a question, choose your lot as god inspires you, and receive the oracle's answer. Reconstructed the full correspondence table and question set from historical sources.

Jewish Calendar Dashboard

A single-page dashboard covering every major and minor Jewish holiday — descriptions, order of observance, and relevant blessings in Hebrew, transliteration, and translation. Uses the Hebcal API to show the next instance of each holiday with candle-lighting times.

Poker Trainer

A drilling app for practicing poker fundamentals. Built with a specific player in mind and iterated on based on their sessions.

Claude Review

A Claude Code skill for recapping recent sessions across projects — what was worked on, what got stuck, what's next. My most PM-flavored project: it exists because I wanted a way to hand off context between sessions and stakeholders cleanly.

Bibliomancy

A Claude Code skill that draws a random passage from a chosen text — a small, playful tool for daily reflection.

Collaborations with Theia Pearson-Vogel

On these I led the PM side — scoping, timelines, playtesting, and making sure we shipped (game jams especially). Also contributed to design, writing, and art.

Sortes Alearum

A webpage simulating an ancient Greek dice-oracle — a kind of ancient fortune-cookie. Divine advice drawn straight from inscriptions found at historical sites in modern Turkey.

The Sacred Text

A short myth-making game / personality test made for GMTK Jam 2019, about the process of redaction that formed the great religious texts and canons. As a would-be god, choose your one true message, and see where it ends up.

The Flock and the Flood

Created for Ludum Dare 51, you must protect and grow your flock of sheep for as long as you can, until the temple their efforts built is all that remains above the tides.

The Fall of Mezentople

Created for Ludum Dare 50, a game about the shape and color of decline. Mezentople will fall — but your choices shape the course. Explore the late years of a fading empire at the end of antiquity, as you make choices affecting the vibrancy and cohesion of eight unique cities.
